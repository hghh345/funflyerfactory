A SMALL SILLY INTERNET PROJECT

THE WORLD DOESN'T NEED MORE UGLY DEEP-FRIED FLYERS.

This project was born purely out of my hatred for AI flyers.

I am not 100% anti-AI, I do believe it has it's role in the coconut tree but, I hate Ai flyers.

Ai flyers are just ugly.

Give me a simple Arial font flyer taped to a lamppost over a deep-fried AI graphic any day.

In an effort to not just sit around finger wagging I decided to make this website. We must be more proactive about being a part of the solution instead of just pointing out the problem.

MAKE SOMETHING YOURSELF.

FunFlyerFactory is a free little tool for making flyers with your own words, your own taste, and as many questionable design decisions as you want. Start with a template or start with a blank page. Make something for your party, your band, your lost cat, your strange idea, or absolutely no reason at all.

It doesn't have to be good. It just has to be yours.
